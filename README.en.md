> **Unofficial** study notes for Stanford CS146S *The Modern Software Developer*, **not affiliated with Stanford University** or the course staff. Course site: <https://themodernsoftware.dev/>

These are notes I take while studying Stanford CS146S (instructor Mihail Eric). Each week has two versions:
- `zh/`: Chinese notes, with key terms and prompts kept bilingual;
- `en/`: the English version of the same notes.

Both versions are **our own paraphrase and commentary**, not the original slides, transcripts or papers. All original materials are linked to their official sources in each week's `links.md`.

## How to read
The notes use three styles to show where content comes from:
- **Body text**: course content. A close rendering of the slides/lecture in their original order, not a word-for-word translation.
- **Blue `[!NOTE]` boxes titled "Supplement"**: supplements, not course content — our own explanations, exercises and extensions.
- **Plain gray blockquotes**: original course quotes with a source — one or two original English sentences + a Chinese translation + the source (e.g. "—— Lecture 1 slides").

Every note starts with an "官方材料 / Official materials" table linking the official slides, video, transcript and readings.

## How to use
For structured study, read the [textbook](book/en/00-contents.md); the steps below are for the weekly notes.
1. Open a week's folder and start with `links.md` to open the official slides / readings;
2. Then read the notes in `en/` or `zh/`; self-checks and hands-on exercises are in the Supplement boxes;
3. For week 1, start with `00_index.md` (Chinese: `00_本周索引.md`).

Layout:
```
weeks/weekNN/
  links.md   links to official materials
  zh/        Chinese notes
  en/        English notes
```
